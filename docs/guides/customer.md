# Customer guide

For people who want to hire a skilled worker.

## 1. Ask for a job

1. Press **New request**.
2. Choose the **service** with the picture tiles, then the exact **skills** if you know them.
3. Describe the problem (at least a few words), choose your **area**, write the **address** and pick a **date** and **time of day**.
4. Press **Find workers**.

Your address is only shown to a worker after they accept your job.

## 2. Choose a worker

The match list shows workers who serve your area and have the skill, best first. Each card says **why** it was suggested (works in your area, has the exact skill, available now, high trust score, verified jobs in this service). Press **Book** on the worker you want.

You can also book from a worker's profile: **Book this worker** opens the request form already linked to that worker.

## 3. Follow the job

Open **My bookings** to see every job and its status:

| Status | Meaning | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| New request | Waiting for the worker (24 h) | Cancel |
| Accepted | Worker took the job — you can now call them | Cancel with a reason |
| On the way / Work in progress | Worker is coming / working | Report a problem |
| Waiting for your confirmation | Worker says the job is done | **Confirm** or **Report a problem** |
| Done | Confirmed — counts as a verified job | Write a review |
| Declined / Expired | Worker could not take it | Choose another worker |

If you do not confirm within 72 hours, the job closes automatically.

## 4. Confirm or report a problem

- **Confirm** only when the work is really finished. This adds a verified job to the worker's history.
- **Report a problem** opens a dispute. Explain what is wrong. The SkillPass team checks the job history and decides; both of you get a notification with the result.

## 5. Write a review

After a job is done, press **Rate this worker** (within 14 days):

- give 1–5 stars for **work quality**, **behaviour**, **on time** and **communication**
- say whether you **would hire again**
- add a comment if you like (no phone numbers)

You can review each job once. Reviews help other customers and change the worker's trust score.

## 6. Notifications

The bell in the top bar shows how many updates you have not read (accepted, on the way, started, please confirm, dispute result). Open **Notifications** to read them, tap one to go to the job, or **Mark all as read**. The list refreshes every 30 seconds.

## 7. Your account

**My account** (`/account`) lets you change your name, email and language, and links to **Change password**. Your phone number cannot be changed; contact support if you get a new number.
