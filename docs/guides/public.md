# Public pages and sign-in

Guide for visitors who have not signed in yet, and for anyone creating an account.

## Find a worker

1. Open the home page. Pick a **service** (AC repair, plumbing, electrical, …) and an **area**, then press **Search**.
2. On **Find workers** (`/workers`) narrow the list with the filters:
   - service, skill and area
   - minimum trust score
   - *Identity verified only* and *Available now*
   - sort by trust score, rating, verified jobs or experience
3. Each card shows the worker's photo, availability, trust score, rating, badges, top skills and areas. Tap the name to open the profile.

## Read a worker profile

The profile (`/workers/<id>`) shows:

| Section | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Badges | **Identity verified** (NID checked by SkillPass), **Skill checked** (per skill, proof reviewed), **N verified jobs** |
| Trust score | 0–100 with a breakdown of 7 parts. Tap **How is this calculated?** for the explainer page. |
| Verified work history | Jobs a real customer confirmed as done, newest first. Customer names are shortened (e.g. "Nusrat J."). |
| Reviews | Overall stars plus quality, behaviour, on time and communication, and "would hire again". |

Identity verification shows **who** the worker is. It is not proof of skill — look at verified jobs and reviews for that.

## How the trust score works

`/trust-score` explains the seven parts: finished jobs, customer ratings, verified jobs, rare cancellations, quick replies, identity check and experience. New workers start from a fair middle value, so a low job count is not a penalty. Nobody can pay to raise a score.

## Create an account

1. Press **Register**.
2. Choose **I need a worker** (customer) or **I am a worker**.
3. Enter your name and Bangladeshi mobile number (`01XXXXXXXXX`).
4. Customers choose a password (at least 8 characters). Workers choose a **6-digit PIN**. Easy PINs such as `123456`, `111111` or the end of your phone number are refused.
5. After registering you go straight to your home screen.

## Sign in

- Enter your mobile number and password or PIN.
- After 10 wrong tries in 24 hours the account is locked. Call support or ask an admin to unlock it.
- **Forgot your PIN?** Call support from the Help screen. An admin can give you a temporary PIN; you must choose a new one right after signing in.

## Report a problem

On a worker profile press **Report this profile**, or **Report** under a review. Choose a reason, add details if you like, and send. You must be signed in. Only the SkillPass team sees who reported.

## Language

Use **English / বাংলা** in the top bar at any time. Bengali is the default.
