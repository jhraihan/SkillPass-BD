# Worker guide (কর্মী গাইড)

For workers, and for the friends, family or SkillPass staff who help them get started. The app is in Bengali by default; button names are shown here in Bengali with English in brackets.

## Bottom bar

Four buttons are always at the bottom: **হোম** (Home), **কাজ** (Jobs), **প্রোফাইল** (Profile), **সাহায্য** (Help). The **🔊** button at the top reads the screen aloud when the phone has a Bengali voice.

## 1. Sign up

1. Press **নিবন্ধন** (Register) and choose **আমি কর্মী** (I am a worker).
2. Enter your name and mobile number.
3. Choose a **6-digit PIN**. Do not use easy numbers like `123456` or the end of your phone number.

If you cannot do this alone, a SkillPass admin can create the account and give you a temporary PIN. Sign in with it and choose your own PIN.

## 2. Set up your profile

The setup wizard asks, one step at a time:

1. **Service and skills** — tap the pictures for the work you do.
2. **Areas** — tap the areas where you take jobs.
3. **Photo** — press **ছবি তুলুন** (Take photo).
4. **Experience** — how many years.

The **প্রোফাইল %** bar on the home screen shows what is still missing. In **প্রোফাইল** you can change everything later. For "About me" you can press **বলে লিখুন** (Speak) and talk instead of typing.

## 3. Be available

On **হোম** turn on **আমি কাজের জন্য প্রস্তুত** (I am ready for work). Turn it off when you cannot take jobs.

## 4. Take a new job

- When a customer books you, the phone **beeps and vibrates** and a **green card** appears at the top: **নতুন কাজ!**
- Press **কাজ দেখুন** (See job). You see the service, area, date and the customer's problem.
- Press the big green **কাজ গ্রহণ করুন** (Accept). The address and phone number appear.
- Cannot go? Press **না, পারব না** (Decline). Please answer within 24 hours — fast answers raise your trust score.

## 5. Do the job

Open the job from **কাজ**. One big button shows the next step:

1. **রওনা দিয়েছি** (On my way)
2. **কাজ শুরু করছি** (Start work)
3. **কাজ শেষ** (Work done)

Use **কল করুন** (Call) and **রাস্তা দেখুন** (Directions) to reach the customer. After **কাজ শেষ**, the customer confirms. If they do not answer in 72 hours, the job closes automatically.

## 6. Get verified

Open **আমার কাগজ যাচাই** (Verify my documents):

- **আমার এনআইডি** — clear photos of the front and back of your NID → **Identity verified** badge.
- **দক্ষতার প্রমাণ** — a certificate or photos of your work for one skill → **Skill checked** mark.

If something is not accepted, the reason is shown and you can send new photos.

## 7. Reviews, work history and trust score

From the home screen open **আমার রিভিউ** (My reviews) to see your trust score parts and what customers said, and **আমার কাজ** (My work) for confirmed jobs. Your score grows when you finish jobs, arrive on time, answer quickly and get good reviews.

## 8. Help

**সাহায্য** has a big **সাপোর্টে কল করুন** (Call support) button, **WhatsApp support**, a tutorial video (if set up) and answers to common questions, each with a 🔊 button.

## For helpers

- Sit with the worker and let them press the buttons themselves.
- Turn on the phone's Bengali text-to-speech (Google TTS) so 🔊 works.
- Add SkillPass to the home screen: browser menu → **Add to Home screen**. It then opens like an app.
